Before we talk

A few straightforward answers to common first questions.

Is Adora a good fit for a small local business?

Yes. We work with startups and local businesses that want practical progress, not a large-agency process. We start with what will make the clearest difference for your business right now.

Can I start with only one service?

Absolutely. Start with social media, Meta Ads, a website, or a custom application. We will recommend a focused first step and add more only when it is useful.

How do the starting ranges work?

The prices shown are editable starting ranges, not a rigid menu. Your final quote depends on the scope, content, ad spend, pages, and integrations involved. We explain it before any work starts.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We will reply by email or phone, usually with a few straightforward questions. If there is a fit, we will suggest a short call and a simple next step.
